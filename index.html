Connection

The page pauses the watch (Ctrl-C) while it works and restarts it a few seconds later. The watch screen may freeze briefly during an operation. That is normal.

The watch logs steps every 6 minutes to logs/YYYY/MM-DD.steps and writes to flash every 3 hours, so the newest data can be a few hours behind unless the step logger can be read live.
